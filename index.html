Qingyang Zhang 张庆阳

I am a 3rd-year PhD student at Tianjin University, supervised by Prof. Changqing Zhang. My research interests include Agentic RL, On-Policy Distillation (OPD) and Synthetic Data / Environment Scaling.

I received my Bachelor's degree from the School of Computer Science, Tianjin University. I then pursued my Master's at the same school and transitioned into a PhD candidate through the direct doctoral program (2+4) in 2024.

Currently, I am a research intern in the Post-train Team of Tencent Hunyuan LLM (Qingyun Talent Program), contributing to Hy4 preview and Hy4. I also spent wonderful time at Alibaba Qwen, Shanghai AI Lab and Tencent AI Lab.
